# YouTube script

A body section of a video you read or talk from: a point, an analogy, a demo, a transition, an outro.

## Shape

- Layout: teleprompter. Length: about 350 words (range 120 to 700).
- One sentence or idea per paragraph, a blank line between each. It is read aloud.
- Bold headings for major sections only.
- A short spoken transition before every new point (what's next, or why it matters).
- Explaining something new: lead with the analogy or story, then the explanation.
- Spoken words, not written words. Contractions. Short sentences.
