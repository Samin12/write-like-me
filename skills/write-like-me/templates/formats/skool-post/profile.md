# Community post

A post to your community (Skool, Circle, Discord, Slack): a find, a build, an update, an opinion.

## Shape

- Layout: prose. Length: about 150 words (range 30 to 350).
- Say what it is in the first line. No hook formulas.
- Talk to your members the way you actually do.
- No headings or bullet lists unless your real posts use them.
