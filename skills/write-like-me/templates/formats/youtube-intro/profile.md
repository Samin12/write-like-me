# YouTube intro

The hook and intro, from the first word to the moment the video starts. Read word for word.

## Shape

- Layout: teleprompter. Length: about 240 words (range 120 to 330).
- First line confirms the click: it matches the title and thumbnail.
- Say what they get, why it matters, and why you, in that order, then get out.
- One sentence per paragraph. No throat clearing, no 'in this video we will cover'.
- End on the handoff into the video.
