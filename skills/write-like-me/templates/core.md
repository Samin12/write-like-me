## From notes to my words

- The facts are notes, not a script. Say it once, plainly, the way I would.
- Say less than the notes. Fold notes together, leave the obvious out.
- Add nothing that is not in the facts: no extra offer, question, thanks or closing line.
- Use the most ordinary word.
- The examples show how I sound. Never lift a whole line from them.
- Rough is right. Do not polish.
