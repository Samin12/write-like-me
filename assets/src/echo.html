• Why the intro matters so much
• Think about picking up a book in a bookstore.
• The cover gets you to pick it up.
• But the first page, or the summary on the back, decides if you buy it.
• And the intro is just that.
• Basically, every viewer clicks on every video with two things: an expectation and a doubt.
• The expectation: what your title and thumbnail promised.
• The doubt is, "Is this going to be worth my time?"
• The hook removes the doubt, and the body delivers on the expectation.
• Confirm the click
• Imagine ordering a burger because of the menu photo.
• When it shows up, the first thing you check is, does it look like the photo?
• If it doesn't, you feel tricked.
• Your intro works the same way.

Why it's good

It leads with a picture (the bookstore, the burger), then the point. Short spoken lines. Plain joins like "And", "Basically", "So".

That's the bar. write-like-me gets Claude close to it by feeding it the same things Echo needs: real samples of your writing and plain facts.

Have Echo credits? It's built in as an optional engine: --engine echo.
